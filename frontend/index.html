The Clinical Challenge

Inpatient discharge does not always signify stable clinical recovery. Unscheduled readmissions within 30 days present significant clinical and operational burdens:

  • Healthcare Penalties: Substantial hospital penalties under readmission reduction initiatives.
  • Capacity Strain: Acute bed occupancy pressure and heightened clinical workload.
  • Patient Vulnerability: Physical and psychological setbacks from avoidable reinstitutionalization.
  • Detection Gaps: Difficulty in identifying multi-morbid diabetic patients without automated telemetry.

The MediPredict Solution

MediPredict bridges electronic health telemetry with an automated machine learning inference pipeline:

  • Instant Lookup: Doctors query by Patient ID; all clinical encounter telemetry loads instantly.
  • 44-Feature Pipeline: Automatic median imputation, categorical encoding, and feature scaling.
  • High Sensitivity: Tuned 0.46 decision threshold achieves 79.03% recall to catch high-risk cases.
  • Actionable Guidance: Provides concrete recommendations including 7-day follow-up and medication checks.

End-to-End Workflow
01 Lookup

Patient ID Query

Clinician enters Patient ID. System queries diabetic_data.csv and retrieves the matching inpatient record.

02 Pipeline

Feature Vectorization

Pipeline isolates 44 model features, replaces '?' with NaN, imputes missing values, and one-hot encodes categorical dimensions.

03 Inference

Ensemble Evaluation

Trained Random Forest ensemble evaluates tree voting to calculate class-1 readmission probability via predict_proba().

04 Action

Threshold & Advisory

Probability is compared against 0.46 threshold; outputs High/Low risk, clinical recommendations, and logs to SQLite.

Decision Threshold Optimization (0.46 vs 0.50)

Standard binary classifiers default to 0.50. In hospital readmission prevention, a False Negative (discharging a patient who will be readmitted without warning) carries far greater risk than a False Positive (scheduling an extra follow-up call).

0.00 (Always Negative) Tuned Threshold: 0.46 Default: 0.50 1.00 (Always Positive)
Low Risk (Class 0)
High Risk (Class 1)
  • Optimization Metric: Tuned via fbeta_score(beta=2) on 3-fold cross validation.
  • Clinical Sensitivity: By choosing 0.46, the model achieves 79.03% Recall (detecting ~8 out of every 10 patients who will return within 30 days).
  • Safety Protocol: Lower threshold catches high-risk diabetic patients who require post-discharge support.
